Where the certified budget is allocated

Indiana DLGF’s 2025 budget order lists three major RBBCSC funds totaling $39,067,151. These are certified budget appropriations—not a claim that every appropriated dollar was ultimately spent.

Education$20,033,800 • 51.3%

Operations$8,971,500 • 23.0%
