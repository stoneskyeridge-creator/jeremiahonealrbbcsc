Why I’m running

A parent first. A board member for everyone.

My family’s experience with special education showed me how important it is to ask questions, speak up and keep looking for better solutions. That brought me to the table, but a board member’s responsibility is to every student, family and employee across RBBCSC.

Campaign priorities

Ask better questions. Build on what works.

I don't have all the answers. I want to understand what RBBCSC already does, listen to the people closest to the work, study proven ideas, measure results and improve where the evidence says we should.

Keep what works. Measure whether it works. Improve what can be improved. Fix what isn't working.
