2027 BUDGET & TAXPAYER OBJECTIONS

This page is provided to make it easier for RBB taxpayers to review the proposed budget information and, if they choose, submit their own objection for possible inclusion in a taxpayer objection petition.

⏰ The window is short

Indiana's Department of Local Government Finance says that 10 or more taxpayers may object to a proposed budget, tax rate, or tax levy by filing an objection petition with the unit's officers no more than seven days after the public hearing. The petition must specifically identify the provisions being objected to.

RBB's public budget hearing was September 15, 2026. The seven-day period ends September 22, 2026.

📄 Review the official information first

Before submitting anything, please review the district's official materials and make your own determination.

📝 You don't have to use this petition

You are free to organize and submit your own taxpayer objection petition. This form is simply here to make the process easier for RBB taxpayers who would like to participate.

You do not have to share the same concern as anyone else. If you use this form, write your own specific objection to a provision of the proposed 2027 budget, tax rate, or tax levy in the space below.

The goal is simple: make the information accessible and give taxpayers an easy way to make their own concerns known during the public-input period.

Before you submit

This form is for objections concerning the proposed budget, tax rate, or tax levy. Please identify the provision you object to as specifically as you can and explain your concern in your own words.

Submitting this form does not itself file an objection petition with RBBCSC and does not guarantee that a submission will qualify for or be included in a formal filing. Responses will be reviewed and, if the statutory requirements are met, may be compiled for timely submission.

Write your own objection

Information submitted for a formal petition may become part of a public filing. Do not include sensitive personal information beyond what this form requests.
